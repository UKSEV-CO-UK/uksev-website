The Fiido C21 is a light step-over e-bike for town streets and mixed paths. A 250W rear hub and a torque sensor add help as you pedal, and the bike weighs 17.5 kg with the battery fitted, so it still feels close to an ordinary bicycle when the motor is off.

Assistance cuts out at 25 km/h and there is no throttle, in line with UK pedelec rules. Three frame sizes cover riders from 160 to 205 cm. The standard battery is a 36V 208.8Wh pack claimed to cover up to 100 km with pedal assist; Fiido also sells an Extender edition with a claimed range of 200 km.
